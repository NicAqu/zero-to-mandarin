Pinyin basics

The four tones

Pinyin spells the sound; the mark over the vowel is the pitch. Tap each card to hear it. Pinyin in this app is coloured by tone.

Neutral tone

Some syllables are short and light, with no mark: ma in 吗, de in 的.

Tone changes you will hear

Two 3rd tones in a row: the first rises. 可以 kě yǐ sounds like ké yǐ.

不 bù before a 4th tone becomes bú: 不是 bú shì.

一 yī becomes yí before a 4th tone (一个 yí gè) and yì before others (一起 yì qǐ).

The app writes the dictionary tones; the audio follows natural speech.
