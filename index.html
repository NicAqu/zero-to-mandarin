Pinyin basics

The four tones

Pinyin spells the sound; the mark above the vowel is the pitch. Tap each card to hear it. Throughout the app, pinyin is coloured by tone.

Neutral tone

Some syllables are short and light with no mark, like ma in 吗 (question particle) or de in 的.

Ear training

You hear a syllable. Pick its tone. 12 rounds.

Two rules you will hear

Two third tones in a row: the first one rises like a 2nd tone. 你好 nǐ hǎo sounds like “ní hǎo”.

不 bù before a 4th tone becomes bú: 不是 bú shì. The app shows the dictionary spelling; the audio follows natural speech.
